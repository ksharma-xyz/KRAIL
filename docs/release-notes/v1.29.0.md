## iOS (App Store)

v1.29.0

Know if you'll get a seat before you board.

1. Long ride home and hoping to sit down? Journey cards and departure boards now show Seats available, Few seats or Standing only next to the time, for trains, Metro and buses that report it.

2. Open a journey card to see every carriage of your train and where the seats are. A line like "Seats expected at the back" tells you which end of the platform to walk to. It keeps updating while the screen is open.

3. On a departure board, tap a train to see the same carriage view. Tracking your trip? The live trip screen shows it too.

4. Not every service shares this yet. When there's nothing to go on, KRAIL shows nothing rather than guess.

5. Fresh stops and routes from Transport for NSW.

Let's KRAIL
