KRAIL / Google Play / Android phone proposal

Seven panels, built from the real captures.

A capture-by-capture evaluation and recommended Android mobile screenshot set. This version uses only the eight PNGs in screenshots/android_mobile, keeps every claim visible or defensible, and treats the images as source captures rather than finished store artwork.

Decision

The set is usable now, but two recaptures would materially improve conversion.

Proceed with seven phone panels. Lead with the daily habit, prove live departures, show KRAIL's unique Park & Ride advantage, warn about broader service disruptions, prove a specific live delay, let riders plan around their day, then close with dark mode. Move the map story to the tablet and iPad sets, where the route and detail pane have enough room to work together.

Capture audit

Good, bad, and the exact next move

Every image below was evaluated at its native 1080 x 2400 resolution. Grades reflect store-marketing usefulness, not the quality of the underlying app feature.

Plan trip sheet

A-

Good: Leave and Arrive are immediately understandable, the clock is visually distinctive, and the screen introduces a benefit not covered elsewhere in the seven-panel set.

Bad: The 9:59 PM value still reads like a test capture, and the dimmed timetable behind the sheet contributes no useful context.

Move: Use as panel 06 with "Plan around your day." A future morning recapture at a recognisable commute time would make it even stronger.

Tallawong parking

A

Good: Distinctive, local and instantly useful. The three car parks, availability counts, occupancy percentages and update times make the benefit tangible.

Bad: The 9:40 PM clock conflicts with any morning-commute or "full by 7:05" headline; the visible data is nowhere near full.

Move: Keep panel 03 with concise "Check parking first" copy. Recapture around 7:00 AM only if a time-specific claim is supported by the visible data.

Bus timetable

A-

Good: The most information-rich proof screen. It shows live departure context, route 333, 15 stops, accessible stops, stands, duration and an onward train connection.

Bad: Dense copy and the long route label lose detail at store thumbnail size. The capture time is late evening.

Move: Keep panel 02. Let the headline carry the benefit and use the full screen as proof rather than expecting every in-app label to remain readable.

Service alerts

A-

Good: Directly addresses disruption anxiety and clearly proves that alerts are tied to a saved Home-to-Work trip.

Bad: The expanded first alert is text-heavy and date-specific, so it will age faster than the rest of the set. The dimmed background is correct for a sheet but reduces colour impact.

Move: Keep panel 04. Refresh this source each release cycle and prefer a concise active alert when one is available.

Light rail timetable

A-

Good: The red mode colour is visually strong and "Delayed 4 mins" is immediate, credible real-time proof. Work and Uni labels also show personalisation.

Bad: It is still a dense timetable screen, so the headline must explain that this is live service status rather than another alerts panel.

Move: Keep as panel 05. Service Alerts covers wider disruptions before travel; this panel proves the live delay on the service the rider is considering now.

Dark mode

B+

Good: Clean, legible and obviously different from the light captures. The orange accent gives the set a strong visual closer.

Bad: It repeats the saved-trips home screen and contains no live operational data. Brown cards against black are less vivid than KRAIL's transport-mode colours.

Move: Keep only as panel 07. The explicit "Dark mode. Your colours." sub-line prevents the screenshot from feeling like an unexplained repeat.

Saved trips

B

Good: Home to Work and Home to Uni communicate repeat use immediately. The uncluttered layout reads well at small size.

Bad: It does not show a live departure, journey status or result, so it cannot honestly support "Save your trip. See it live." Large empty areas reduce perceived feature depth.

Move: Use truthful "Save your trips" copy for now. Highest-priority recapture: open a saved trip and show live status while preserving the recognisable Home-to-Work context.

Journey map: phone source

C

Good: Sydney geography, the L2 route and Central Chalmers Street create useful local recognition.

Bad: The whole map appears greyed by an overlay, the bottom sheet cuts off its useful details, and only part of the route is visible. At thumbnail size the background becomes visual noise.

Move: Exclude from the phone set and rebuild as a native tablet/iPad panel. Use the wider canvas for a full route map plus a persistent journey-detail pane, with no dim layer and both endpoints visible.

Production fixes

Three changes before final export

Recapture the lead screen

Show a saved Home-to-Work trip with live status, departure time or disruption state. The first screenshot needs both the habit and the payoff.

Align clock and claim

Either capture the set in the 7:00 to 7:30 AM commute window or remove time-specific morning claims. Visible evidence must agree with the headline.

Render real 9:16 exports

Compose the 1080 x 2400 captures into the framework's 1080 x 1920 Play canvas, verify safe borders, and export seven final PNGs from one manifest.

Recommended approval: approve the seven-panel order and copy direction shown above, with panels 01 and 03 marked for recapture before upload.

Do not use "Tallawong often full by 7:05am" with the current 9:40 PM, 64%-full capture. Do not use "See it live" on the current saved-trip home screen. Both are stronger claims than the screenshots prove.
