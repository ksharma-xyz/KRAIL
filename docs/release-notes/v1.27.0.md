# KRAIL v1.27.0 — Release Notes

## Android — Google Play Store

v1.27.0

🔍 Search that forgives punctuation: type "central chalmers" without the comma and KRAIL still finds the stop.

📡 Back on its own: lose signal in a tunnel and KRAIL refetches the moment you surface. No pull to refresh.

🔒 Search text now helps us fix results that come back wrong. Numbers are masked on your phone before anything is sent, and it is never tied to you.

Fixed: Show previous departures now lands where it should.

Let's KRAIL.

## iOS — App Store

v1.27.0

1. Search forgives punctuation. Type "central chalmers" without the comma and KRAIL still finds the stop.

2. Back on its own. Lose signal in a tunnel and KRAIL refetches as soon as you surface, without a manual refresh.

3. Search text and your privacy. The text you type into stop search is now kept, so we can fix the searches that come back wrong. Every number is masked on your phone before anything is sent, and it is never tied to your identity. Full detail at krail.app/privacy-policy.

4. Latest NSW timetables, stops and routes.

5. Fixed: Show previous departures now anchors correctly, and service alerts stay put when the list updates.

Let's KRAIL.
