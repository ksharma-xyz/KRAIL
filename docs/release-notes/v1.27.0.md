## iOS (App Store)

v1.27.0

You can talk to KRAIL now.

1. Hands full on the platform? Tap the mic in stop search and just say where you're going. It's for the moments when typing is a pain: you're carrying shopping, it's raining, or the train is two minutes away and you're still on the stairs. Speak the start and type the rest if that's quicker. Your phone does the listening itself wherever it can, so the audio stays on your phone.

2. Searching for a stop is easier than ever. You don't have to type a stop name exactly the way it's written. Get it close and KRAIL finds it.

3. Lose your signal in a tunnel? KRAIL reconnects automatically the moment you're back above ground, so your timetable is already waiting. No pulling the screen down, no wondering whether the times you're looking at are real.

4. Fresh timetables, stops and routes from Transport for NSW.

5. Fixed: tapping Show previous departures now leaves you where you were reading instead of jumping, and service alerts stay put when the list refreshes.

Let's KRAIL
