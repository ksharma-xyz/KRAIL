# KRAIL v1.30.0 — Release Notes

## Android — Google Play Store

v1.30.0

⚠️ Buses replacing your train or metro? KRAIL now says so. When your next trip has a replacement bus, a warning card sits above your results and each replaced trip is labelled. Tap it for the Transport NSW alert and when it runs, or More info to open it.

Seat tags now read "Seats at one end" instead of guessing front or back.

Fresh stops and routes from Transport for NSW.

## iOS — App Store

v1.30.0

Know why your trip looks different.

1. When buses replace a train or Metro service on your next trip, a warning card now appears above your trip results, and each replaced trip is labelled "Replacement bus".

2. Tap the card to see the Transport for NSW alert and when it applies.

3. More info opens the alert on the Transport for NSW website.

4. The card only appears when there is an alert to open, so it never shows without a reason.

5. Seat levels now read "Seats at one end" instead of naming the front or back of the train, until the car order is confirmed.

6. Fresh stops and routes from Transport for NSW.
