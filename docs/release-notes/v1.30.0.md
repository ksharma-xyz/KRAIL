# KRAIL v1.30.0 — Release Notes

## Android — Google Play Store

v1.30.0

⚠️ Metro disrupted? KRAIL now says so. When buses replace metro, a warning card sits above your trip results with what's affected and when. Tap it for the last metro services before the buses start, or More info for Transport NSW.

Seat tags now read "Seats at one end" instead of guessing front or back.

Fresh stops and routes from Transport for NSW.

## iOS — App Store

v1.30.0

Know why your metro trip looks different.

1. When buses replace Metro, a warning card now appears above your trip results, so the odd bus and train options make sense. It says what is affected and when.

2. Tap the card to open it. Times and stops are in bold, with the last Metro services before the buses start listed underneath.

3. More info opens the alert on Transport for NSW.

4. Metro only for now. Trips that do not start and end on the Metro show nothing extra.

5. Seat levels now read "Seats at one end" instead of naming the front or back of the train, until the car order is confirmed.

6. Fresh stops and routes from Transport for NSW.

Let's KRAIL
