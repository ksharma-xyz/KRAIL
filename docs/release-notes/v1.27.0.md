## iOS — App Store

v1.27.0

1. Speak your trip. Tap the mic in stop search and say where you are going. Your words are added to the field, so you can speak part of it and type the rest. Transcription happens on your phone wherever your device supports it.

2. Search forgives punctuation. Type "central chalmers" without the comma and KRAIL still finds the stop.

3. Back on its own. Lose signal in a tunnel and KRAIL refetches as soon as you surface, without a manual refresh.

4. Search text and your privacy. The text you type into stop search is now kept, so we can fix the searches that come back wrong. Every number is masked on your phone before anything is sent, and it is never tied to your identity. Full detail at krail.app/privacy-policy.

5. Latest NSW timetables, stops and routes.

6. Fixed: Show previous departures now anchors correctly, and service alerts stay put when the list updates.

Let's KRAIL.
