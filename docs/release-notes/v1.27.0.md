## iOS (App Store)

v1.27.0

You can talk to KRAIL now.

1. Say it instead of typing it. Tap the mic in stop search and tell KRAIL where you're going. It's for the moments when typing is a pain: you're carrying shopping, it's raining, you're already half way down the stairs and the train is in two minutes. Speak the start and type the rest if that's quicker. Your phone does the listening itself wherever it can, so the audio stays on your phone.

2. Search is more forgiving. You don't have to type a stop name exactly the way it's written any more. Get it close and KRAIL finds it.

3. Better on patchy signal. KRAIL handles a dropped connection properly now. Lose signal underground and it reconnects on its own the moment you're back above ground, so your timetable is already waiting. No pulling the screen down, no wondering whether the times you're looking at are real.

4. Fresh timetables, stops and routes from Transport for NSW.

5. Fixed: tapping Show previous departures now leaves you where you were reading instead of jumping, and service alerts stay put when the list refreshes.

Let's KRAIL
